Add MPay screenshots

  1. In MPay open Transactions, pick the month, tap All.
  2. Screenshot from the top down to the last transaction you already added. Overlapping screenshots are fine.
  3. Pick them below and tap Read on this phone. Every line is checked against MPay's balance before anything is added.

Home Screen widget

Shows safe-to-spend, your limits and this week's spending on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, using Scriptable, a free app.

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store.
  2. Tap Copy widget script. In Scriptable tap +, paste, and name the script Ledger Widget.
  3. Tap Update widget. Then touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, add Scriptable, tap the widget and choose Ledger Widget.

Backup

Your changes are saved on this phone only. Clearing Safari data or switching phones erases them, so save a backup after each import.
